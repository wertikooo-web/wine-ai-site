Today's reality

Wine knowledge is scattered across a dozen places

Every winery has its own website, its own Instagram, its own listing on an aggregator. A visitor is left with ten open tabs and no single consistent answer.

A winery website in one language, with no word on what's actually in stock
An Instagram page with prices buried in three-year-old comments
A paper map of tasting rooms handed out at the airport
A host who speaks neither English nor Chinese
What's inside

One knowledge base, every screen and format

At the core is a living knowledge base of Moldovan winemaking: winery history, wine and vintage profiles, terroir, grape varieties, serving rules, food pairings, tastings, events, prices and purchase links.

The main interface, Wine AI, holds a natural voice or text conversation in several languages — from a winery's story to a single bottle's serving temperature and food match.

Who it's for

Two ways to look at one platform

For a winery

A digital employee who never takes a day off

Tells the estate's story, hosts guests in their own language during high season, takes load off staff, and guides visitors toward a purchase or a tasting booking — even when every table is full.

For a guest

One guide across the whole of Moldovan wine

Helps plan a route across regions, makes sense of an unfamiliar native grape, and keeps the relationship with the wine going long after the trip — from the first question to a bottle at home.

Honest about the stage

We're building the foundation, not promising a finished product

WINE AI today is a working prototype of a conversational sommelier — not a shipped production system. Before we show this to wineries and partners as a finished solution, we're earning stability in the base conversation.

  • Now

    Prototype & stabilization

    Knowledge-base search quality, response speed, barge-in handling, reliability of voice providers.

  • Pilot

    The first real user

    Not a launch — daily use: one winery, one wine shop, or a partner such as WineMD.

  • Scale

    New formats

    A desktop device, White Label, an SDK for third-party integrations — once demand and unit economics are proven.

Platform principle We don't replace sellers, sommeliers or winery staff — we become their helper at peak hours, in languages no one on staff speaks, and at the moments when the team is stretched thin.
